The Setup Process

Healthy Water. Happy Life.

Every thriving aquarium moves through the same four stages — here's what to expect at each one.

01

Set Up

Choose a tank size, substrate and hardscape suited to the fish.

02

Cycle

Establish beneficial bacteria over 4–6 weeks before introducing.

03

Balance

Dial in temperature, pH and filtration, then stock gradually.

04

Thrive

Maintain a steady rhythm of water changes and observation.
